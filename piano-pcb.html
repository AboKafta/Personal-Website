07 PCB DESIGN

Piano PCB

A piano circuit from an Arduino beginner’s guidebook, turned into a custom PCB with a Nano in place of the Uno and a Murata piezo buzzer.

01Goal

The goal was to reinforce the basics of PCB design. I opened my Arduino beginner’s guidebook and chose the Piano project, which comes with a breadboard prototype image, a schematic, and the code.

02Schematic

In KiCad, instead of connecting everything with wires and copying the reference exactly, I used labels. The other differences from the reference are swapping the Arduino Uno for an Arduino Nano and using a buzzer symbol for the piezo. The piezo’s footprint, symbol, and 3D model came from DigiKey; everything else came from KiCad’s built-in library.

My first drawing had issues like duplicate and mismatched labels, and ERC reported a power output error. I added a power flag on the Nano’s ground pin to mark it as supplying power. With the errors cleaned up, the schematic was done.
